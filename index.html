THE IDEA

The deadline stays.
The hour moves.

Flexible tasks like EV charging or a dishwasher do not always need to start immediately. Switch Hour checks each five-minute start time that still meets your deadline, totals the forecast emissions over the full run, and selects the lowest result.

01
Real grid signal

California Energy Commission MIDAS supplies WattTime's regional marginal emissions signal. It estimates the emissions response to additional electricity demand.

02
A fair comparison

The app assumes the same total kWh and even power use in both windows. It compares the forecast, not your entire household footprint or actual measured savings.

03
Your choice, your schedule

It never controls your device. Add the suggested time to your calendar if it works for you.
